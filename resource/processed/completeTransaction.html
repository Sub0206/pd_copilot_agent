CoreSuite NAPC : completeTransaction

Created by Former user, last modified on Dec 05, 2019

Mark Transaction as Closed

DescriptionMarks the policy transaction identified by the current context as Closed. Specifically updates question 40 on entity 30,000, assumed to be Policy Status.
Supported Action TypeStandard
ConstraintsMust be executed from within a Policy Transaction context.
Suggested DependenciesPolicy Status = Open
removal_code <> P (Pending Removal) or R (Removed)
NotesThis method is not typically used by itself as an action, but as a step in a more complex Policy Action.
This step is most often used as a step in the Issue Policy Action.
