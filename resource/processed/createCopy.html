CoreSuite NAPC : createCopy

Created by Former user, last modified on Dec 05, 2019

Copy to new Master Contract

DescriptionCopies all aspects of the policy from the current Policy Transaction context to create a new quote or application based upon whether the policy being copied is a quote or not.
The new policy will be version 1 of that policy and will have no relationship to the policy that was copied.
Upon creation of the new policy, the context of the action is changed to the new policy's transaction.
Supported Action TypeStandard
ConstraintsMust be executed from within a Policy Transaction context.
Suggested Dependenciesremoval_code <> P (Pending Removal) or R (Removed)
Notes

The new policy will have the text "- Copy" appended to the name field to identify it as the copy.

THIS METHOD IS NOT PRODUCTION-READY - DO NOT USE (There are known issues that make the results suspect.)  For test data generation purposes only.
