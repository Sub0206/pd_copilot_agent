Created by Former user, last modified on Dec 05, 2019

Policy Number Assignment

DescriptionGenerates a policy number by prefixing "I-" to the beginning of the Quote or Application number and specifically updating question 160 on entity 30000, assumed to be the Policy Number.

Typically used for localized installations or demos.
Supported Action TypeStandard
ConstraintsMust be executed from within a Policy Transaction context.
Suggested DependenciesPolicy is not Issued
Policy Status = Open
removal_code <> P (Pending Removal) or R (Removed)
NotesNone
