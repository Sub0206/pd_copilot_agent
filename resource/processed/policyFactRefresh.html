Policy Fact Refresh

DescriptionThis method will refresh the Policy Facts held in memory for the Policy Transaction Version in context.
Supported Action TypeStandard
ConstraintsMust be executed from within a Policy Transaction context.
Suggested DependenciesNone
NotesAfter the Policy Facts are refreshed in memory, if there are Questions or Dependencies that use the Policy Facts, then a Performed Configured Views may be needed afterwards to pull the refreshed Policy Facts.
