CoreSuite NAPC : runPaymentMonitor

Created by Former user on Oct 25, 2019

Run Payment Monitor

DescriptionThis method will use the provided attributes to create tracking events for a given policy based on the current standing of the policy (overdue) and its previously (or lack of) tracking events.
Supported Action TypeStandard Action
ConstraintsMust be executed from within Adaptik Billing
Suggested DependenciesNone
NotesNone


XML Usage

Generate from Database

Attributes

There will be 15 attributes to either determine which payment monitor version to use (External Only or Internal Only) or to set the amount of days to trigger specific events.

CodeLiteralValueDescription
monitorVersionFree Form TextA or B

Option A - Payment Monitor will not create External Warning events. 

Option B - Payment Monitor will only create External Warning events.

overdueBalTolAmtUSDFree Form textNumber (1 – 999)Minimum past due amount in USD that the system will recognize as a "Payment Issue".
overdueBalTolAmtCADFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Minimum past due amount in CAD that the system will recognize as a "Payment Issue".
directCollectDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days from Collection Attempt to alerting for Direct Collection
attemptCollectDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days from Collection Letter to alerting for Collection Attempt
letterCollectDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days from Collection Invoice to alerting for Collection Letter
cancel2CollectDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days left between Cancel Notice & Collection Invoice
collectInvDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days overdue to trigger a Collection Invoice Event
extWarn2CancelDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days left between External Warning & Cancel Notice events
cancelNoticeDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days overdue to trigger a Cancel Notice Event
intWarn2ExtWarnDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days overdue to trigger a Cancel Notice Event
extWarnDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days overdue  to trigger an External Warning Event
nearExpDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Maximum number of days from Current Date to Policy Expiration Date for policy to be considered "Near  Expiration"
intWarnDaysFree form textNumber (1 – 999)Number of days overdue to trigger an Internal Warning Event
